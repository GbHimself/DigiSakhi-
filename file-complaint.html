File a Complaint

Know exactly where to report every type of online crime — step-by-step guides, official government portals, applicable laws, and emergency helplines.

Financial Fraud / UPI Scam

Lost money via UPI, fake calls, task scams, or fake shops

1
Call 1930 within 30 minutes

The Cyber Crime Helpline can freeze the fraudster's bank account in real-time if you report fast enough.

2
Screenshot all transaction details

Note: date & time, amount, UPI ID or phone number, bank transaction ID from your payment app.

3
File at cybercrime.gov.in

Click "File a Complaint" → "Financial Fraud" → select the type → fill in your details and upload screenshots.

4
Visit your nearest Cyber Cell

Bring your phone, screenshots, and bank statement. Ask for an FIR under IT Act Sec.66D or BNS Sec.318.

5
Complain to your bank too

Email or call your bank's fraud helpline. Request a freeze on the destination account and ask for a chargeback if possible.

Time Matters Most

Bank freeze requests work best within the first 2 hours of fraud. After 24 hours it becomes much harder to recover money.

Laws That Protect You
  • IT Act Sec.66D — Cheating by impersonation using a computer
  • BNS Sec.318 — Cheating & dishonest delivery of property
  • BNS Sec.316 — Criminal breach of trust
Open cybercrime.gov.in Call 1930 Now

Morphed / Fake Photos & Blackmail

Someone edited your photo or is threatening to share fake images

1
Do NOT pay the blackmailer — ever

Paying once leads to more demands. Save all messages and screenshots as evidence instead.

2
Report on cybercrime.gov.in

Click "Women / Child Related Crime" → "Morphed Images" or "Blackmail / Sextortion".

3
Report on the platform itself

Tap the three dots → "Report" → "Fake or Misleading". Platforms remove reported content quickly.

4
Call 1930 or Women Helpline 1091

Both helplines will connect you to a cyber cell officer and guide you through next steps.

5
Set all profiles to Private now

Lock down your Instagram and Facebook. Delete public photo albums temporarily.

You Are Legally Protected

Sharing morphed or intimate images without consent is a serious criminal offence. The accused can be sentenced to 3 – 7 years in prison.

Laws That Protect You
  • IT Act Sec.67 — Publishing obscene material electronically
  • IT Act Sec.67A — Publishing sexually explicit content
  • BNS Sec.351 — Criminal intimidation / blackmail
  • POCSO Act — Extra protection if victim is a minor
Report Morphed Images Call 1091 (Women Helpline)

Cyber Stalking & Online Harassment

Someone keeps messaging, threatening, or following you online

1
Screenshot everything BEFORE blocking

Messages with timestamps are your primary evidence in court. Screenshot first, then block.

2
File on cybercrime.gov.in

Click "Women / Child Related Crime" → "Cyber Stalking / Bullying Against Women and Children".

3
Go to your nearest police station

Cyber stalking can be reported at any police station. Ask for a written receipt of your complaint.

4
For workplace harassment — use SheBox

Go to shebox.wcd.gov.in, register, and file a complaint against the specific person and organisation.

Workplace Harassment?

File on shebox.wcd.gov.in — a government portal built specifically for women facing workplace sexual harassment.

Laws That Protect You
  • BNS Sec.78 — Stalking (up to 5 years imprisonment)
  • BNS Sec.79 — Voyeurism
  • IT Act Sec.66E — Violation of privacy
  • POSH Act 2013 — Workplace sexual harassment
cybercrime.gov.in SheBox — Workplace Complaints

Account Hacking & Data Leak

Your WhatsApp, email, or social media was taken over, or your personal data was leaked

1
Recover your account first

WhatsApp: re-register with your own number. Email/Instagram: use "Forgot Password". Enable Two-Step Verification immediately.

2
Warn all your contacts

Tell your family and SHG group immediately. Anyone who got suspicious messages from your account should NOT send money.

3
File on cybercrime.gov.in

Click "Other Cyber Crime" → "Hacking / Unauthorised Access". Provide your account details and timeline.

4
For Aadhaar data leak — contact UIDAI

Visit uidai.gov.in or call 1947 (toll-free) to report misuse of your Aadhaar details.

Prevent It Happening Again

Change all passwords, turn on Two-Step Verification on every app, and never share OTPs — even with people claiming to be from tech support.

Laws That Protect You
  • IT Act Sec.43 — Penalty for unauthorised computer access
  • IT Act Sec.66 — Computer-related criminal offences
  • IT Act Sec.72 — Breach of confidentiality & privacy
  • DPDP Act 2023 — Digital Personal Data Protection
Report Hacking Aadhaar Data Leak — UIDAI

Romance Scam / Fake Profile

You were deceived by someone pretending to be a friend, doctor, or army officer online

1
Stop all contact immediately

Do not send any more money. Block the person on all platforms. Save all screenshots before blocking.

2
Call 1930 if money was sent

Bank accounts can still be frozen if reported within hours.

3
Report the fake profile on the platform

Facebook/Instagram: go to the profile → three dots → Report → "Pretending to be Someone".

4
File on cybercrime.gov.in

Click "Women / Child Related Crime" → "Fake Profile / Impersonation" → upload screenshots.

5
Speak to someone you trust

Call iCall free counselling: 9152987821. You are not alone and not at fault.

You Are Not at Fault

These are professional scammers who deceive thousands of people every year. Reporting helps protect you and prevents the same person from hurting others.

Laws That Protect You
  • IT Act Sec.66C — Identity theft
  • IT Act Sec.66D — Cheating by impersonation using a computer
  • BNS Sec.318 — Cheating & fraud
Report Fake Profile Call 1930 Now
