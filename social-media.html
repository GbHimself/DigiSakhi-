WhatsApp Safety Rules

Do NOT forward messages about politics, religion, or unverified news. Check facts at factcheck.afp.com or boomlive.in first.

Do NOT add unknown people to your personal contacts or SHG group chats.

Do NOT click links sent by unknown numbers, even if they look like government sites.

Enable Two-Step Verification: Settings → Account → Two-step verification → Enable

Turn on "Disappearing Messages" in private chats for extra privacy.

Regularly check which devices are connected to your WhatsApp (Settings → Linked Devices).

How scammers lure women into Telegram groups

💰 Easy Earning Group

Our members have earned ₹50,000+ this month! 🎉
How do I join?
Complete first task → Get paid → Then invest ₹2,000 to earn ₹800/day! 🚀
SCAM: Real jobs never ask you to invest money to earn money
⚠ First payment is bait — they always ask you to invest next
⚠ "Invest ₹X to earn ₹Y" is ALWAYS a scam — no exceptions
✅ Real earning opportunities never require you to pay first
✅ Report to 1930 immediately if you already sent money

Using UPI & Google Pay Safely

UPI is India's fastest payment system — but also the most misused by fraudsters. These screenshots show exactly what to watch for.

The Collect Request Scam

Most common UPI fraud targeting women sellers

Google Pay
💳 Payment Request PAY ₹1,500
From: 98765XXXXX (Buyer)
"Sending money for saree"
PAY ₹1,500
Decline
⚠ This button SENDS money — do NOT tap PAY!
⚠ This notification means YOU are being asked to PAY — not receive
⚠ "Sending money for saree" is a LIE — it's a collect request
✅ To receive money — you do NOTHING. No notification needed
✅ Always tap DECLINE on unexpected payment requests

QR Code Safety

Scanning the wrong QR code can send your money away

Scan QR Code
⚠ Before scanning any QR:
✅ Check: Shopkeeper's name shown?
✅ Check: Is the amount shown before paying?
❌ If QR asks for YOUR fingerprint/PIN = fraud
❌ If QR was sent on WhatsApp by stranger = fraud
✅ Legitimate QR codes only RECEIVE money — they never ask for your PIN to "receive"
✅ Only scan QR codes at physical shops you can see
✅ Confirm the merchant name shown matches the shop
⚠ Never scan QR codes sent to you on WhatsApp or SMS
⚠ No QR code should ever ask you to enter your UPI PIN to receive money

Fake Cashback & Lottery SMS

You did NOT win ₹10 lakh on Google Pay

SMS from +91-8888XXXXXX
🎉 Congratulations! Your Google Pay number has won ₹10,00,000 in the Annual Cashback Lucky Draw!

To claim, send ₹199 processing fee to UPI: claim@ybl

Offer valid till midnight only! 🕐
SCAM: Google Pay does NOT run lotteries. Processing fees = fraud
⚠ Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm never announce winners by SMS
⚠ Any fee to "claim a prize" is a scam — always
✅ Delete and block the number immediately
✅ Report to 1930 if you already sent money
Topic 3

Stopping Fake News

Fake news spreads fear and causes harm. Learn how to identify and stop it.

Signs of Fake News

  • Headlines with "SHOCKING!", "BREAKING!", or "VIRAL"
  • No source or author mentioned
  • Requests you to "Forward to everyone NOW"
  • Creates fear, panic, or strong emotion
  • Very bad grammar and spelling errors
  • Photos that don't match the story

How to Verify News

  • Check if the same news is on a known TV channel or newspaper
  • Visit PIB Fact Check (pib.gov.in/factcheck) for government news
  • Search the headline on Google to find multiple sources
  • Check the date — old news is sometimes shared as current
  • Reverse image search photos on Google Images
  • If unsure, do NOT forward — silence is safer

Legal Consequences

  • Forwarding fake news is a criminal offence in India
  • You can be arrested even if you did not create the news
  • Penalties include fines and jail time under IT Act
  • Fake news about COVID, religion, or elections is especially serious
When in doubt, DON'T forward. Think before you share.
