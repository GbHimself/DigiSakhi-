Government Schemes for Women

Know your rights and benefits under these important schemes

PM Jan Dhan Yojana

Free bank account with zero balance, RuPay debit card, and accident insurance. Every woman should have a Jan Dhan account linked to her Aadhaar.

Helpline: 1800-11-0001 pmjdy.gov.in
Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana – NRLM

National Rural Livelihood Mission provides financial support, training, and credit linkage for women's Self-Help Groups. Register your SHG at your local bank or Block office.

Helpline: 1800-180-5999 aajeevika.gov.in
Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana

Pregnant and lactating mothers get ₹5,000 as direct cash benefit in three instalments. Apply at your local Anganwadi centre or health sub-centre.

Helpline: 104 wcd.nic.in
PM Ujjwala Yojana

Free LPG gas connection for women from BPL households. Over 9 crore connections given. Apply at your nearest LPG distributor with Aadhaar and BPL card.

Helpline: 1906 pmuy.gov.in
PM Awas Yojana – Gramin

Financial assistance to build a pucca house in rural areas. Priority is given to women-headed households. Apply through your local Gram Panchayat.

Helpline: 1800-11-6446 pmayg.nic.in
Pradhan Mantri Gramin Digital Saksharta Abhiyan

Free digital literacy training for rural household members. Learn how to use smartphone, internet, and digital payments. Contact your CSC (Common Service Centre) to enroll.

Helpline: 1800-3000-3468 pmgdisha.in
Take Action

How to Report Cyber Crime

Call 1930

The National Cyber Crime Helpline. Available 24/7. You can report financial fraud, online threats, and any cyber crime by calling this number.

Call 1930

Online Report

Visit cybercrime.gov.in to file a detailed complaint online. You can report financial fraud, women/child-related crimes, and more.

Visit Website

Visit Police Station

Go to your nearest police station and ask to file a cyber crime complaint. Take screenshots of messages and any evidence with you.

What Evidence to Collect

Screenshots of threatening messages
Phone numbers of the fraudster
URLs / links used in the scam
Bank transaction details and references
Email screenshots if any
Date and time of incident
Free Training

Where to Get Free Digital Training

Common Service Centres (CSC)

CSC centres in your village or block provide free digital literacy training. They also help with government scheme applications, Aadhaar services, and more.

How to find: Search "CSC near me" on Google Maps or ask at your Gram Panchayat office.

Block Resource Centres

NRLM Block Resource Centres provide training for SHG members on digital payments, banking, and livelihood skills. Ask your SHG facilitator for training dates.

Online on Your Phone

Watch free tutorials on YouTube in your language. Search for "smartphone kaise chalaye" or "UPI payment kaise kare" to find simple video tutorials.

PMGDISHA Centres

Under PM Gramin Digital Saksharta Abhiyan, free certified digital literacy courses are available. Contact your nearest CSC or call 1800-3000-3468.
