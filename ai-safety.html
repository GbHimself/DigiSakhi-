AI Safety & Awareness

Artificial intelligence can be a helpful tool — but it is also being misused to cheat, blackmail, and harm women. Know both sides.

Ways AI Makes Life Easier for Women

These are real, free tools available on your phone right now.

Language Translation

Google Translate and voice apps help you understand messages in different languages, making communication easier for SHG members across India.

Try: Google Translate app — free on Play Store

Voice to Text

WhatsApp voice messages can be converted to text, helping you understand messages even in noisy environments or when you cannot play audio.

Try: Hold the microphone icon on your keyboard to type by speaking

Google Lens — Smart Search

Point your camera at any text, product, plant, or medicine to instantly get information. Very useful for SHG members checking product prices or reading labels.

Try: Open Google app → tap the camera icon

AI in Banking Apps

Banking apps use AI to detect suspicious transactions and alert you instantly. They also show spending patterns to help you manage your SHG budget better.

Enable transaction alerts in your banking app settings

Health Information

Apps like Practo and mFine use AI to help you understand symptoms, find nearby doctors, and get basic health guidance — helpful in rural areas with limited access.

Try: Practo or 1mg app — free to use

Learning & Skills

YouTube's AI recommends free tutorials in your language for skills like stitching, cooking, farming, and small business — helping SHG women grow their income.

Search YouTube: "SHG business ideas Hindi" for free videos

How Criminals Misuse AI Against Women

These are real crimes happening across India right now — with screenshots showing exactly what they look like. Knowing them protects you.

AI DANGER #1

Deepfake Videos

AI places your face on someone else's body — looks completely real

Video Player 0:08 / 0:47
AI GENERATED
0:08 / 0:47
Scammer sends this to victim: "Pay ₹50,000 or I share this with your family"
How to Spot It
Face edges look blurry or unnatural
Blinking looks wrong or missing
Lighting on face differs from background
Lip movement does not match the words
Real Case — Bengaluru 2025: A woman's face was placed on an inappropriate video using a free AI app. She was blackmailed for ₹50,000. When she paid, demands doubled to ₹1 lakh. She finally reported to cybercrime.gov.in — accused arrested in 18 days.
Never pay. Report to 1930 immediately. Police can trace the source.

Real Case — Hyderabad 2025: SHG leader saw ₹1.8 lakh in her task app balance. When she tried withdrawing, it asked for a ₹20,000 "tax fee." She paid. App asked for ₹35,000 more "insurance." She paid again. App disappeared. Total lost: ₹2,00,000.
Any app that asks you to PAY to withdraw your own earnings is a 100% scam. Close it immediately and call 1930.

Always verify Aadhaar at uidai.gov.in. A photo of an ID card means nothing — demand a live video call instead.

6 Steps to Protect Yourself from AI Scams

Simple actions you can take today — no tech knowledge needed.

1

Verify Emergency Calls — Always

If someone calls claiming to be a family member in an emergency, hang up and call them back on their usual saved number. Ask one question only they would know. Never send money based on a voice call alone.

2

Set All Social Media to Private

Go to Instagram and Facebook settings → Privacy → set profile to Private. Remove old public photo albums. AI needs your photos to create fakes — fewer public photos means less material for scammers.

3

Demand a Live Video Call Before Trusting Anyone Online

AI chatbots and deepfake operators cannot do real-time live video calls easily. If someone refuses to do a live video call after weeks of chatting — they are not who they claim to be.

4

Never Send Money to Someone You Have Not Met in Person

No matter how long you have chatted, how many photos or videos they have sent, or how caring they seem — never transfer money to someone you have never physically met. This rule has no exceptions.

5

If Blackmailed — Do Not Pay, Report Immediately

Paying a blackmailer once always leads to more demands. Take screenshots of all messages, block the person, and immediately report to cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930. Police can track and arrest the accused.

6

Trust Your Instincts

If a voice sounds slightly "off," if a video looks unnaturally smooth, or if a conversation feels too perfect — trust that feeling. It costs nothing to verify. It can save everything to be cautious.
