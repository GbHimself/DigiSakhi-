Stay Safe

Quick Safety Tips

Simple rules to keep yourself safe in the digital world

01

Lock Your Phone

Always set a PIN, pattern, or fingerprint lock on your phone to prevent others from accessing your data.

02

Never Share OTP

No bank, government office, or company will ever ask for your OTP. Never share it with anyone — even family.

03

Guard Personal Info

Do not share your Aadhaar number, bank details, or address with unknown people online or on phone.

04

Think Before You Click

Do not click links from unknown messages. Fraudsters send fake links to steal your information and money.

05

Download Safely

Only download apps from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. Avoid apps from unknown websites.

06

Keep Apps Updated

Update your phone and apps regularly. Updates fix security problems and keep your phone safe.
