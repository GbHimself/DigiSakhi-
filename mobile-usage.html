Mobile Usage

Learn how to use your smartphone step by step — calls, WhatsApp, camera, payments, and more. No experience needed.

Making Calls and Sending Messages

How to call, save contacts, and send SMS

Make a Phone Call

1

Tap the Phone app (green icon) on your home screen

2

Tap the keypad icon and type the number

3

Press the green call button to call

4

Press the red button to end the call

To call a saved contact — tap Contacts, search the name, tap the phone icon next to their name.

Save a Contact

1

Open the Contacts app

2

Tap the + (Add) button

3

Type the person's name and phone number

4

Tap Save

Save important numbers like 1930 (Cyber Crime), 1091 (Women Helpline), and your SHG leader's number.

Send an SMS

1

Open the Messages app

2

Tap the compose (pencil) icon

3

Type the phone number or select a contact

4

Type your message and tap the Send arrow

Never click links that come in SMS from unknown numbers — they could be scams.

Block a Number

1

Open the Phone app and go to Recents

2

Tap and hold the number you want to block

3

Tap Block / Report Spam

4

Confirm — the number can no longer call you

Block all numbers that call asking for OTP, Aadhaar, or bank details immediately.

Using Your Phone Camera

Take photos, scan documents, and use Google Lens

Take a Clear Photo

1

Tap the Camera app on your home screen

2

Point the camera at what you want to photograph

3

Tap the screen to focus on your subject

4

Press the round shutter button to take the photo

For SHG product photos — use natural daylight, clean background, and hold the phone steady for sharp images.

Scan Documents

1

Place your document flat on a table in good light

2

Open Camera and hold it directly above the document

3

Make sure all 4 corners are visible in the frame

4

Take the photo — you can send it on WhatsApp or save it in DigiLocker

Use Google Drive or DigiLocker to store digital copies of your Aadhaar, ration card, and bank passbook.

Google Lens — Identify Anything

1

Open the Google app and tap the camera icon in the search bar

2

Point it at any text, plant, product, or medicine

3

Google will instantly identify it and show information

Use Google Lens to read labels on medicine bottles, identify plants, check product prices, or translate text in any language.

Scan a QR Code

1

Open your phone camera and point it at the QR code

2

Hold it steady — a link or information will appear on screen

3

Tap the link only if you know and trust where it leads

Never scan QR codes sent to you on WhatsApp by unknown people — they can take money from your UPI account.

What You Can Do With Your Mobile

Your smartphone is more powerful than you think — here are useful things it can do for you every day

Pay Bills & Transfer Money

Use Google Pay, PhonePe, or your bank app to pay electricity bills, mobile recharge, and send money to family — instantly, 24/7, no bank visit needed.

Learn New Skills Free

Watch free tutorials on YouTube in Hindi and regional languages — cooking, stitching, pickle-making, farming, government schemes, and more.

Health & Medical Help

Use Aarogya Setu to find nearby hospitals, book appointments, and check vaccination certificates. Use 1mg or Practo for medicine information.

Store Your Documents

DigiLocker lets you store Aadhaar, ration card, birth certificate, and bank passbook digitally — accepted by government offices. Free and official.

Navigate Anywhere

Use Google Maps to find any location — hospitals, banks, government offices, markets. Works offline if you download your area map in advance.

Sell Your SHG Products

Take photos of your handicrafts, pickles, or stitched items and share on Facebook Marketplace, WhatsApp groups, or Meesho to reach buyers across India.

Read News in Your Language

Apps like Dailyhunt and ShareChat show news in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, and 14 other Indian languages — stay informed about government schemes and local news.

Get Emergency Help Fast

Save 1930 (cyber crime), 1091 (women helpline), 108 (ambulance), and 100 (police) in your contacts. One tap in an emergency can save lives.

Know Your Phone Settings

Important settings every woman should know — privacy, notifications, accessibility and more

Privacy Settings

1

Go to Settings → Privacy

2

Check which apps have access to your Camera, Microphone, Location, and Contacts

3

Turn OFF access for apps that don't need it (e.g. a game doesn't need your microphone)

4

Check Location — set to "Only while using app" for navigation apps

If an unknown app asks for access to your contacts or camera — say NO and uninstall it.

Manage Notifications

1

Go to Settings → Notifications

2

You will see a list of all apps sending notifications

3

Turn OFF notifications for apps you don't need updates from

4

Keep ON: WhatsApp, banking apps, government apps

Too many notifications drain your battery and distract you. Keep only important ones ON.

Change Phone Language

1

Go to Settings → General Management → Language

2

Tap Add Language and search your language (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, etc.)

3

Select it and set as default — your whole phone will switch to that language

Using your phone in your own language makes it much easier and more comfortable to use.

Fingerprint & Face Lock

1

Go to Settings → Security → Biometrics

2

Tap Fingerprint or Face Recognition

3

Follow the instructions to register your fingerprint or face

4

Now you can unlock the phone with one touch — faster and safer than PIN

Add your own fingerprint only — don't add anyone else's without your knowledge.

Accessibility — Bigger Text & Voice

1

Go to Settings → Accessibility

2

Tap Font size and style → increase to Large or Huge

3

Turn on TalkBack if you want the phone to read out everything aloud

4

Turn on Display size to make icons and buttons bigger

Bigger text is very helpful for elders or anyone with difficulty reading small text.

Child / Guest Mode

1

Some phones have Kids Mode — search it in Settings

2

You can restrict which apps children can use

3

Set a PIN so children cannot exit Kids Mode without your permission

Kids Mode prevents children from accidentally making calls, spending money, or accessing the internet unsafely.

Digital Wellbeing

Use your phone in a healthy and balanced way — protect your eyes, sleep, and time

Check Your Screen Time

1

Go to Settings → Digital Wellbeing and Parental Controls

2

You will see a dashboard showing how many hours you used your phone today

3

It shows which apps you used the most

4

Set a daily timer on apps that distract you — phone will remind you when time is up

Awareness of screen time helps you use your phone more purposefully and reduce unnecessary use.

Bedtime Mode & Sleep Better

1

Go to Settings → Digital Wellbeing → Bedtime Mode

2

Set a bedtime schedule (e.g. 10 PM to 6 AM)

3

The phone screen turns grey and silences notifications so you sleep better

Avoid using your phone 30 minutes before sleeping. Blue screen light reduces sleep quality.

Protect Your Eyes

  • Turn on Night Light / Eye Comfort — Settings → Display → Night Mode. Makes screen warmer and easier on eyes at night.
  • Keep the phone at arm's length — not too close to your face
  • Follow the 20-20-20 rule — every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds
  • Do not use phone in complete darkness — always have some background light

Healthy Mobile Habits

  • Use your phone for productive things first — learning, banking, communication
  • Keep mealtimes and family time phone-free
  • Do not forward messages without reading and verifying them first
  • Do not compare your life with what you see on social media — most is not real
  • If using phone feels addictive or stressful — take a 1-day break each week

Mobile for SHG & Small Business

Use your phone to grow your SHG, sell products, manage money, and reach more customers

Sell on Facebook Marketplace

1

Open Facebook → tap Marketplace (shop icon at bottom)

2

Tap + Sell → Item for sale

3

Add photos of your product, name, price, and description

4

Tap Next → Publish — your listing is now visible to buyers in your area

Take bright, clear photos of your products — good photos get more buyers. Use natural daylight.

Promote on Instagram

1

Create a separate business account on Instagram for your SHG products

2

Post photos with a short description and price in caption

3

Use hashtags: #HandmadeIndia #SHGProducts #WomenEntrepreneur

4

Ask buyers to message you directly to place an order

Post regularly — even 3 photos a week can bring new customers. Stories disappear in 24 hours so use them for daily updates.

Collect Payments via UPI

1

Open Google Pay or PhonePe → find your UPI ID (looks like name@ybl)

2

Share your UPI ID with customers for payment

3

Or show your QR code — tap "Receive Money" to get your QR

4

You will get an SMS and notification when payment arrives

To RECEIVE money — share only your UPI ID or QR code. Never enter your PIN to receive. Never approve a "collect request" from a buyer.

Track SHG Savings with Apps

1

Download Google Sheets (free) from Play Store

2

Create a sheet with member names, monthly savings, and loans

3

Share it with your SHG leader using "Share" button — everyone can see the same sheet

4

All changes sync automatically — no need for paper registers

Google Sheets is free and works in Hindi. It auto-calculates totals so no math errors in savings records.

Sell on Meesho

1

Download Meesho Supplier from Play Store

2

Register with your phone number and bank details

3

List your products with photos and price

4

Meesho delivers to buyers all over India — you just pack and hand over to courier

Meesho is used by thousands of SHG women across India to sell sarees, handicrafts, and home-made food products nationwide.

Run SHG Meetings on WhatsApp

  • Create a WhatsApp group for your SHG — add all members
  • Share meeting date, venue, and agenda on the group in advance
  • Collect monthly savings digitally via UPI and record in Google Sheets
  • Share government scheme updates from official sources only
  • Use WhatsApp video call for members who cannot attend in person
